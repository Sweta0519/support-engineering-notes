# Support engineering notes

Playbooks, checklists and infographics about technical support work in B2B SaaS, by Sweta Sahoo, Senior Customer Support Engineer. Eight years in support queues, most recently at Sauce Labs, working with Postgres, REST APIs, logs and the customers who depend on them.

Everything here started as a LinkedIn post. The text is the long version; the image is the version that fits a feed. Corrections and counter-arguments are welcome as issues.

Topics:
- **Playbooks**: repeatable procedures for triage, escalation and debugging.
- **Tools**: open-source and AI tools that make support teams faster, with honest limits.
- **Metrics**: what FRT, TTR, CSAT and friends actually measure.
- **Career**: what senior support engineering looks like and how to get there.
- **Lessons**: short stories with a takeaway.
